How to cook an egg, in steps

  1. Bring the water to a boilEnough to cover the egg by a finger’s width
  2. Lower the egg in with a spoonStraight from the fridge is fine
  3. Six and a half minutes for a soft yolkNine for one that is set
  4. Into cold water for a minute, then peel
