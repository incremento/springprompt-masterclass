Before people arrive

  1. Open the slides on the laptop and the remote on your phone. The dot at the top left of the remote turns green.
  2. Scan the audience code with a second phone (or a private tab) and step through each live slide. Answer once to see the result draw on the slide.
  3. On the remote: More → New session clears the test answers.
  4. Keep the laptop awake and on mains power. If the venue wifi blocks devices from seeing each other, use a public link (see the hosting notes in the skill).
